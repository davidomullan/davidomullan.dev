A practical background, built across different worlds.

I grew up in the suburbs of Chicago and earned a B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from The Ohio State University. After college, I served four years as a U.S. Naval officer, stationed in Yokosuka, Japan and Coronado, California.

That experience shaped how I approach work: stay curious, communicate clearly, take ownership, and keep moving when the problem is unfamiliar. I am bringing that mindset back to software development and looking for work where technical judgment and real-world impact matter.
